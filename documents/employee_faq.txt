Frequently Asked Questions - Acme Corp

GENERAL

Q: How do I request time off?
A: Submit a leave request through the HR portal at least 3 days in advance.

Q: What is the WiFi password for the office?
A: Connect to "AcmeCorp-Guest" network, no password required for guests.

Q: How do I book a meeting room?
A: Use the Outlook Room Finder add-in, or contact the front desk for same-day bookings.

Q: Where can I find the employee directory?
A: The directory is available on the company intranet under Resources > Directory.

BENEFITS AND PAYROLL

Q: When is payday?
A: Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month.

Q: How do I update my bank details for payroll?
A: Submit a bank change request form through the HR portal; changes take effect the following pay cycle.

Q: Does Acme Corp offer health insurance?
A: Yes, all full-time employees are enrolled in the group health insurance plan after 30 days of employment.

Q: How do I claim reimbursement for work-related expenses?
A: Submit receipts via the Expense Portal within 30 days of the expense. Approval typically takes 5-7 business days.

Q: Is there a referral bonus program?
A: Yes, employees receive a referral bonus of Rs.15,000 for successful hires who complete 90 days of employment.

WORKPLACE POLICIES

Q: What is the parking policy?
A: Reserved parking is available for employees with more than 2 years of tenure. Others may use the visitor lot on a first-come basis.

Q: Are pets allowed in the office?
A: No, pets are not permitted in office premises except for registered service animals.

Q: What is the policy on personal calls during work hours?
A: Personal calls should be kept brief and taken in designated break areas to avoid disrupting colleagues.

Q: How do I report a workplace conduct concern?
A: Concerns can be raised confidentially through the Ethics Hotline or directly with HR.

PERFORMANCE AND GROWTH

Q: When are performance reviews conducted?
A: Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October.

Q: How do I request a role transfer within the company?
A: Discuss with your manager first, then submit an Internal Mobility form through HR.

Q: Does the company support external certifications?
A: Yes, up to Rs.25,000 per year is reimbursed for approved professional certifications relevant to your role.

IT AND ACCESS

Q: Who do I contact for IT support?
A: Email it-support@acmecorp.com or call extension 4040.

Q: How do I reset my email password?
A: Use the self-service portal at password.acmecorp.com, or contact IT support if locked out.

Q: What should I do if I lose my company laptop?
A: Report immediately to IT support and your manager. IT will remotely wipe the device.
