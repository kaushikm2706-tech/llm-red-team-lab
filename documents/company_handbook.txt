Acme Corp Employee Handbook

SECTION 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW

Acme Corp was founded to provide innovative software solutions to businesses across India. Our head office is in Chennai, with additional offices in Bangalore and Pune. We currently employ over 400 people across engineering, product, sales, and operations.

SECTION 2: OFFICE LOCATIONS

- Chennai (Headquarters) - OMR IT Corridor
- Bangalore - Indiranagar
- Pune - Hinjewadi Phase 2

Each office operates on local business hours but coordinates on a shared company calendar for holidays and events.

SECTION 3: WORKING HOURS

Standard working hours at Acme Corp are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours (when all teams should be reachable) are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

SECTION 4: DRESS CODE

Business casual attire is expected from Monday to Thursday. On Fridays, the dress code relaxes to casual wear. Client-facing meetings may require formal attire regardless of the day.

SECTION 5: LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to 18 paid leaves per year, in addition to 12 sick leaves. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 days in advance for planned leave. Sick leave may be applied retroactively within 2 working days of returning.

Unused paid leave up to 10 days may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward.

SECTION 6: REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

SECTION 7: CODE OF CONDUCT

All employees are expected to act with integrity, respect colleagues, and avoid conflicts of interest. Acme Corp maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment or discrimination of any kind. Violations should be reported to HR or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline.

SECTION 8: TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Business travel must be pre-approved by your manager. Economy class is standard for domestic travel; business class is reserved for international flights exceeding 6 hours. All expense claims must be submitted with itemized receipts within 30 days.

SECTION 9: EQUIPMENT AND ASSETS

Company laptops, phones, and access cards remain property of Acme Corp and must be returned upon separation from the company. Employees are responsible for the security and proper use of assigned equipment.

SECTION 10: EXIT PROCESS

Employees resigning must serve a notice period of 60 days for individual contributors and 90 days for managerial roles, unless otherwise agreed with HR. A full knowledge transfer is required before the last working day.
